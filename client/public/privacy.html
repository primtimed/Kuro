Privacy Policy

Kuro · last updated 29 September 2026

Kuro is a private media app used by a single household. It is not offered to the public; only people invited by the household admin can sign in.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

  • Your Google account ID and email address, to recognise you and link you to your profile.
  • No password, contacts, files or other Google data. Kuro only requests your basic profile.

What Kuro stores while you use it

  • Your watch history, favourites, likes and favourite live TV channels, per profile.
  • Your signed-in devices (a name such as "Chrome on Windows" and when it was last used).
  • An optional profile PIN, stored only as a one-way hash.

How it is used and shared

This data is used only to run Kuro for the household. It is stored on the household's own server, is never sold, and is not shared with anyone. Kuro does not use advertising or tracking.

Removing your data

Ask the household admin to remove your profile or unlink your Google account. Signing out of a device, or removing it under Settings → Devices, deletes that device's sign-in immediately. You can also revoke Kuro's access at any time from your Google account's security settings.
